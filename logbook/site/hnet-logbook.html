EXP-000 · Cartesia Fig. 7 re-analysis

2026-10-28 · status: done · cost: $0 · script: logbook/exp000_reference.py

Question. How much of the blog's ">10x fewer tokens" comes from the architecture, and how much from comparing a cooled-down H-Net with a stable-phase SmolLM3?

Data. The per-checkpoint scores behind blog I Fig. 7, extracted from the page's chart bundle into configs/reference/blog1_fig7_{hnet,smollm3}.csv: * the H-Net has 154 points to 0.99T tokens (cooldown from 0.855T); * SmolLM3 has 117 points to 11.04T (stage 1 up to 7.93T).

The six tasks are HellaSwag, ARC, PIQA, WinoGrande, CSQA and MMLU.

Method. Fit SmolLM3's stage-1 (stable-LR) average as a quadratic in log tokens. For each stable-phase H-Net point, find the SmolLM3 token count that reaches the same score.

Results.

H-Net tokens H-Net avg SmolLM3 avg at same tokens SmolLM3 tokens to match (smoothed) Data multiplier
94B 55.66 53.87 162B 1.7x
189B 58.11 56.86 560B 3.0x
301B 58.74 — 822B 2.7x
0.99T (after cooldown) 63.82 — 11.04T (SmolLM3 final, after stages 2–3) 11x

Takeaways. * The architecture effect at matched stable phase is real but ~2–3x, not 10x. The H-Net's cooldown adds ~2.3 points (61.5 → 63.8). * Our pass criteria for any H-Net vs baseline comparison use stable-vs-stable multipliers. Cooldown is a separate, explicitly labelled branch. * Cartesia's SmolLM3 Winogrande (63.69 at the end) differs from the model card (58.88), so their eval setup differs from the card. Our harness is validated against these CSV numbers, not the card.
