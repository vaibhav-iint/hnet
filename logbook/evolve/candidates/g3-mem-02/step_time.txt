# Wall-clock step time of the fast trie head (topk 64) vs the full head (g1-mem-01) and the parents, CPU, same batch.
# The machine is a shared 4-core box with three other proposers' jobs (load average 5-13 during these runs), so every
# comparison is interleaved (round r times one step of every arm) and reported as the median over rounds.
#
# Summary (median step-time ratios):
#   L03, hnet_ws parent (load ~5, 1 x 8512 bytes, 5 rounds):  full head (g1-mem-01) 1.341x | fast head k=64 1.137x
#                                                            (k=32 0.986x, k=16 1.001x: within noise of the parent)
#   L03, hnet_ws parent (load ~9, 5 rounds):                 full head 1.313x | fast head k=64 (g3-mem-01) 1.100x
#   L03, g2-match-mem parent (load ~9, 5 rounds):            g3-mem-02 (fast head k=64) 1.013x (min-to-min 1.117x)
#   Target: < 1.3x the parent's step time on the L03 config -> met (1.10-1.14x for g3-mem-01, 1.01-1.12x for g3-mem-02).
#
# On the A100 the head is a larger share of the step than on CPU (the full head took 1.88 vs 1.02 s mean step, 1.84x,
# vs 1.31-1.34x on CPU). Projection from the head-alone micro-benchmark (head time as a fraction x of the full head's,
# A100 step = 1.02 + 0.86 x s): k=64 -> 1.18x (load ~5) to 1.25x (load ~9) of hnet_ws, k=32 -> 1.10-1.20x. The
# projection assumes GPU cost scales like CPU cost; the full head also re-gathers under activation checkpointing
# (3 gather passes vs 2), so it overstates the fast head's GPU cost if anything.
